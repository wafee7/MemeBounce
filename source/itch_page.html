Re:bound

RE

In RE:BOUND, every failure sends you bouncing back, turning your past mistakes into the key to victory.

Controls

  • Move: A / D or ← / →
  • Jump: Space / W 
  • Rebound: R (fail on purpose)
  • Pause: Esc / P
  • Gamepad: Left Stick, A, Y, Start

How to Play

  • Fail to progress: Every death sends you bouncing back!
  • Use your echoes: Your previous attempt becomes a shadow that repeats your actions.
  • Solve puzzles: Use echoes to activate pressure plates and open doors.
  • Fail intentionally: Press R to create an echo exactly when you need one.
  • One echo at a time: Every new failure replaces the previous echo.
  • Bounce around: Use springs and bonk flying meme cats to launch yourself!
